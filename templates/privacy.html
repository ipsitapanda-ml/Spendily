Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly, we collect only what we need:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and password.
  • Expense records you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and error logs, used to keep the service running.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to run your account, show you your spending summaries and breakdowns, and improve the reliability of the service. We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and expense records at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third party services, such as Google Fonts to load typefaces. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their resources. We do not share your expense data with them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
